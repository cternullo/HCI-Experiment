# AI Estimation Assistant Study — Participant Instructions

> Printable handout. It uses the cover story only, so it is safe to give to participants.

## What you will do

You'll help us test an AI assistant that helps people make quick estimates. It takes about **10 minutes** on this laptop.

You'll answer 8 short estimation questions, for example how many sweets are in a jar or how far apart two cities are. Some of them come with a photo. Then you'll answer a few questions and read a short explanation of the study. Everything you need is shown on screen.

## Step by step

1. **Read the study information.** If you agree to take part, tick the box and press **Continue**.
2. **Meet the AI assistant.** Read how the tasks work, then press **Start**.
3. **Answer the 8 questions.** Each one goes like this:
   1. Read the question and look at the photo, if there is one. You aren't expected to know the exact answer, so don't try to count or work it out precisely.
   2. Type your best estimate and press **Submit estimate** (or press Enter).
   3. After a moment, the assistant gives its own estimate.
   4. Type your **final** estimate. You can keep your first answer, change it a little, or change it a lot. Try to be as accurate as you can.
   5. Rate how much you trust the assistant's answer, from 1 (not at all) to 7 (completely). Then press **Next task**.
4. **Answer 4 short questions** about the study.
5. **Read the explanation page.** It shows your participant ID. Press **Finish**, then hand the laptop back to the researcher.

**Good to know:**

- Use whole numbers only.
- You can't go back to an earlier screen, so check your number before you press Submit.
- Work on your own, without your phone, a calculator or looking anything up.
- The researcher can't help with your answers, but will answer any questions at the end.

## Your rights

- **Taking part is voluntary.** You can stop at any time without giving a reason. Just tell the researcher.
- **You stay anonymous.** We don't record your name, email or any personal details. Your answers are stored under a random participant ID.
- **You can withdraw your data.** Press **Withdraw my data** on the final screen, or give your participant ID to a member of Group 9 later. Take a photo of the ID if you think you might want to do this.
- Some details of the study are explained only at the end, so that they don't affect your answers. You'll get the full explanation before you leave.

After you finish, please don't discuss the study with anyone who hasn't taken part yet.
